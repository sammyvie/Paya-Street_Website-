About Paya Street

Paya Street Coffee is a small-scale, community-centered coffee shop that opened its Angeles City branch between mid-2023 and 2024. Known for its minimalist interior and inviting atmosphere, Paya Street was founded by Lianna, Marty, and Lester Wayne Evangelista. Their vision? To make premium-quality drinks more accessible and enjoyable for everyone — especially students and young professionals.

Our Story

More Than a Cup

At Paya Street, we don't just serve coffee — we build community. Whether you're sipping your go-to latte or wearing your favorite tee, Paya Street is all about comfort, creativity, and connection.

Our merch line — from cozy shirts to minimalist caps and playful stickers — is designed to extend the Paya experience beyond the café. Purpose meets style. Wherever you are, take a piece of Paya with you.

Style That Speaks

Every item in our collection is inspired by our space — minimalist, welcoming, and made for moments. We believe your style should feel just as good as your first sip in the morning: effortless and intentional.
